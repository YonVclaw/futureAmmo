[h1]Change — Class names now carry their faction[/h1]

Magazine and ammo class names now start with their side — [b]FA_b_[/b] (BLUFOR), [b]FA_o_[/b] (OPFOR), [b]FA_i_[/b] (Independent) — and compat magazines carry their pack's tag (FA_jca_, FA_rf_, FA_lot_, ...). Easier to script against and to filter in the arsenal.

[b]Nothing breaks:[/b] every old class name still works — saved loadouts, mission crates and scripts using the old names keep functioning, and both names are visible in the arsenal for this release.

[h1]Fixes — Scorcher/Sochor Artillery, Mk200 Belts, Katiba Magazines[/h1]

[h2]Fixed[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Vanilla 155mm artillery broken[/b] — the Scorcher and Sochor lost their gun and spammed the RPT log with "No entry mortar_155mm_AMOS" errors. Our vehicle ammo was attaching to the 155mm weapon before the game had defined it; it now loads in the right order. If you had unusable SPGs, they work again.
[*][b]Mk200 (Stoner) could load MX SW magazines[/b] — the belt-fed Mk200 was offering 100-round MX SW box mags. Those 100-round loads now sit where they belong, on the MX SW itself, and the Mk200 only takes proper 200-round belts.
[*][b]Katiba magazine errors[/b] — the Katiba's magazine well listed a dozen caseless tracer mags that never existed, so filling a CSAT unit logged "creating magazine … with scope=private" errors. The dead entries are gone; the working EPR tracer mags are untouched.
[/list]

[h1]MAAWS Accuracy Fix & Field Manual Update[/h1]

[h2]Fixed[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]MAAWS rounds falling short[/b] — the unguided HEAT 665 CS and HE 448 AB left the tube slower than the launcher's sight was calibrated for, so they dropped well below the aimpoint (a 400 m target impacted around 250 m). [*][b]Airburst controls explained[/b]: the programmable-airburst keybind (40mm Mk364 / MAAWS HE 448 / Vorona Oskol) is unbound by default — the Overview now says where to bind it and lists the range steps.
