== Balance: 6.5x39 caseless repositioned between 5.56 and 7.62 ==

The 6.5 family never sat where an intermediate cartridge should. It hit for the
same 8 as 5.56 while carrying a 7.62-class bullet, and its drag/BC figures were
flatter than 7.62 outright (Mk329 BC 0.32, XM893 0.34 vs XM751's 0.245), so 6.5
shot further than the round it is supposed to sit under. Penetration was
scattered in both directions — Mk328 pierced like 5.56 (2.4) while XM892/XM893
out-pierced every 7.62 load (4.2 / 4.6).

Every West 6.5 stat is now the arithmetic midpoint of its 5.56 and 7.62 role
peer (Mk327/M80A2 for GP, XM891/XM751 for CTEP), so the caliber reads as a true
step between the two — ~2.9-3.3 kJ muzzle energy against 1.8-1.9 kJ (5.56) and
3.9-4.2 kJ (7.62). No classes were added, renamed or removed.

  hit / caliber / typicalSpeed / airFriction / BC / bullet mass g, old -> new:
    Mk330 EPR   8/2.8/855/-0.00095/0.290/8.0   -> 11/2.7/950/-0.00104/0.190/6.4
    Mk328 HV   11/2.4/810/-0.00055/0.28 /8.0   -> 11/2.8/965/-0.00101/0.196/6.4
    XM892 CTEP 12/4.2/830/-0.00050/0.29 /7.8   -> 12/3.0/970/-0.00099/0.204/6.5
    Mk331 AP    8/3.4/840/-0.00090/0.300/8.4   -> 11/3.3/945/-0.00100/0.201/6.6
    Mk329 LR   13/2.6/860/-0.00045/0.32 /9.07  -> 12/2.9/960/-0.00095/0.212/6.8
    XM893 CTEP 13/4.6/880/-0.00042/0.34 /8.9   -> 12/3.2/980/-0.00092/0.219/6.9

Role spread inside the band: Mk331 AP keeps the +0.6 pen offset that 5.56's
Mk332 carries over its GP round (2.7 -> 3.3) and stays the slowest load;
Mk328/XM892 are the carbine loads (330/407/508 mm); Mk329 LR and XM893 CTEP are
the 610 mm DMR/MG loads at the top of the band. deflecting now follows the
tier its peers use (GP 15, CTEP 14, LR 13, AP 12) and tracerScale is 0.7
throughout — midway between 5.56's 0.6 and 7.62's 0.8.

The four matrix loads (Mk328/XM892/Mk329/XM893) also gained the ACE fields the
rest of the small-arms line already carried and they were missing entirely:
ACE_muzzleVelocityVariationSD, ACE_velocityBoundaries, ACE_standardAtmosphere,
deflecting, tracerScale, and real multi-point ACE_muzzleVelocities /
ACE_barrelLengths curves instead of a single 508 or 610 mm data point.

CSAT's 6.5 trio keeps its documented x0.90 gradient against the rebalanced West
peer (speed and pen scaled, geometry/BC copied from the parent), so the faction
gap is unchanged:
    Type 41 EPR  7/2.5/770 -> 10/2.4/855   (<- Mk330)
    Type 42 CT  11/3.8/745 -> 11/2.7/873   (<- XM892)
    Type 43 AP   7/3.1/755 -> 10/3.0/851   (<- Mk331)
Type 42 also picks up the 3-point barrel curve its parent uses (was a single
508 mm point).

213 magazine initSpeed values across ammo/CfgMagazines.hpp, ammo/CfgMag65_matrix.hpp
and ef/CfgMagazines.hpp were resynced to the new muzzle velocities.

Untouched: the 6.2x40 caseless line (csat62 DBP-25/26/88B, DBJ-25 PAB) stays
exactly where it was, and the Mk367 PAB keeps inheriting vanilla 6.5 ballistics
and its balance-tuned fuze envelope.

== New: rifle anti-drone buckshot — Mk368 / Mk369 AD (K/L) ==

NEW — antidrone: rifle-cased tungsten buckshot for FPV/quad intercept with no
fuze script — a pure config submunition burst at the muzzle (poisson-disc with
a center pellet, same mechanism as the 12 ga Mk360 AD and 40mm Mk389 TBK).
No tracer variants. Pattern holds a 1 ft radius at each variant's effective
range (coneAngle = atan(0.3048 / range)):
  - 5.56x45 Mk368K AD — 8 pellets,  eff. 100 m, 671 m/s (2200 fps)
  - 5.56x45 Mk368L AD — 5 heavier pellets, eff. 200 m, 671 m/s
  - 7.62x51 Mk369K AD — 12 pellets, eff. 150 m, 640 m/s (2100 fps)
  - 7.62x51 Mk369L AD — 10 heavier pellets, eff. 250 m, 640 m/s
Pellet drag is tuned so every variant keeps ~45-47% of muzzle velocity at its
max effective range; past that the pattern goes soft fast. Mags feed the
STANAG 5.56 (30Rnd) and M14 7.62 (20Rnd) wells; the caliber diary and the

== Change: 155mm SPG shells now ride the ACE_155mm_artillery magazine well ==

ghostfa_vehicles no longer reopens the vanilla 155mm gun to attach its shells. The
25 FA 155mm magazines are injected into the CBA magazine well ACE_155mm_artillery
(new addons/vehicles/CfgMagazinewells.hpp) instead of magazines[] += on
mortar_155mm_AMOS.

That well is defined by ACE3's ace_missile_clgp, which is also what attaches it to
the gun (mortar_155mm_AMOS: CannonCore { magazineWell[] += {"ACE_155mm_artillery"}; }).
Vanilla mortar_155mm_AMOS carries no magazineWell[] of its own, so ace_missile_clgp
was added to requiredAddons — without it the 155mm rounds would not appear at all.

Consequences:
  - We stop touching mortar_155mm_AMOS entirely; only a forward declaration remains,
    so weapon_ShipCannon_120mm can still name it as its parent. This retires the
    parentless-reopen hazard that previously collapsed the destroyer gun and the RF
    twin mortar (both inherit AMOS) to scope=private.
  - weapon_ShipCannon_120mm inherits AMOS and declares no magazineWell[] of its own,
    so the Mk45 Hammer now also lists the 155mm shells. Pre-existing ACE behaviour
    (their M712 rounds already ride along via the same inheritance). The Hammer's own
    FA 120N shells are unchanged and still attached via magazines[] +=.
  - 82mm (mortar_82mm), 230mm (rockets_230mm_GAT) and the 120mm naval gun stay on
    magazines[] +=: neither vanilla nor ACE/CBA defines a magazine well for any of
    them, so a well would still require reopening the weapon to attach it.

Swept the rest of the file for the same parentless-reopen hazard the AMOS fix was
about and found three more, now all naming their real vanilla parent (verified
against a derapified weapons_f.pbo, all defined in A3_Weapons_F):

== Fix: ship cannon + RF twin mortar collapsed to scope=private (shared AMOS parent) ==

Two weapons loaded with scope=private and no model/simulation/muzzles, flooding
the RPT with "No entry '.../<weapon>.scope/.type/.muzzles/...'" + "Error: creating
weapon <weapon> with scope=private": the vanilla destroyer gun
weapon_ShipCannon_120mm and the Reaction Forces twin mortar Twin_Mortar_120mm_RF.

In-game Config Viewer shows both share one parent chain:
  weapon_ShipCannon_120mm -> mortar_155mm_AMOS -> CannonCore -> Default
  Twin_Mortar_120mm_RF    -> mortar_155mm_AMOS -> CannonCore -> Default

The cause was ghostfa_vehicles reopening mortar_155mm_AMOS PARENTLESS (class
